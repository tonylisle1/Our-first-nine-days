For my little Dove

Before there was a memory, there was a moment.

I thought I was flying across the world to finally meet the woman I loved. I didn't know that in just nine days, I'd discover a thousand little things I never wanted to live without.

These are the days the way I remember them. Just us—and everything you made me feel.

With all my love,
Tony

Our first chapter

Nine days. One beginning.

DAY 01 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

The Day You Became Real

I remember searching the airport crowd, hoping to find you. Then there you were. After so many calls, messages, good mornings, and good nights, the woman I loved was standing right in front of me. We hugged, and all those miles that had separated us suddenly seemed to disappear. What stayed with me was your hand in mine. Such a simple thing, and something I had waited so long to experience.

DAY 02 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

A Place for Me

I had come to see you, but you were also opening the door to your world. Meeting Benj and the people you loved mattered to me. I brought little pieces of home—gifts, chocolates, and something for Benj—but the greatest gift was finally being there, no longer just a voice or face on a screen.

DAY 03 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

The People Who Love You

Meeting your family in Batangas made the trip about more than just the two of us. With every introduction, I learned more about the people who helped make you who you are. I came for you, Dove. I began to understand that loving you also meant caring about the people you love.

DAY 04 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

A Promise

The promise ring was small enough to fit in my hand, but the meaning I placed in it was so much bigger. I wasn't thinking about a temporary holiday romance. I wanted to choose you when I was standing beside you, and to keep choosing you when I was back across the ocean.

DAY 05 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

Words for the Days Apart

I knew the goodbye would eventually come, so I brought my “Open When” letters. I wanted you to have something real you could hold when all we had again were calls and messages. Those little envelopes were my attempt to reach across the distance before it returned.

DAY 06 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

The Joy of Being Us

Some of my happiest memories weren't planned. They were your playful expressions, the way you teased my half-smile and eye rolls, the dancing, and the way we could laugh without needing any grand occasion. I loved discovering the everyday version of us.

DAY 07 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

Becoming a Family

At Forest Cove, with Benj and your family around us, something shifted in me. I remember the beach, the water, the singing, the laughter, and you in your black dress. I wasn't just visiting a place anymore. I was imagining the life I wanted with you and Benj.

DAY 08 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

One More Ordinary Day

I wanted another morning, another swim, another quiet moment beside you. After Forest Cove, we had time at RMS. I kept wishing I could slow the hours down. The remarkable thing was how badly I wanted even the most ordinary day with you.

DAY 09 · MARCH 30 – APRIL 7, 2026

Until I Could Hold You Again

The final day came whether I was ready or not. There was coffee, Jollibee, time together, and the drive toward Manila. I gave Benj my Ohio State hat and asked him to look after his mom. At the airport, goodbye came in a way neither of us wanted. I left with so much love, and with the certainty that I wanted to come back.

The little things

The memories between the memories.

How you called me fluffy, like a teddy bear. How you teased that half-smile and rolled your eyes back at my eye rolls. The way you held my hand, held onto my arm, and made even the quietest moments feel like the ones I never wanted to end.

I discovered something on that trip: sometimes all I want is to hold you and have nowhere else in the world I need to be.

Until the next chapter

Those first nine days were never enough. But they were the beginning of something I still choose, every day.

Mahal kita, my little Dove.

— Tony
