My Dove, I wanted to give you something more lasting than a collection of vacation photographs. I wanted to remember what it felt like to finally hold you, to learn your world, to become part of your family for a little while, and to discover how much the ordinary moments could mean.

This is my side of our first nine days. Not every minute was photographed. Not every conversation can be reconstructed word for word. But these are the memories I carried home, in the order I lived them, and the feelings I never want to lose.

For you. For us. For the day we can look back together and remember how it all began.
